---

## The idea

Players rate each other. A card’s public **OVR** is the average of ratings teammates gave that player. Those peer OVRs are what the app uses to split fair teams.

You cannot rate until you **claim your own card**. Claiming is once only.

---

## How the score works

Every rating uses seven sliders (each **25–95**):

| Stat | What it means |
|------|----------------|
| Pace | Speed |
| Shooting | Finishing |
| Passing | Distribution |
| Dribbling | Close control |
| Defending | Tackling / marking |
| Physicality | Strength / aerial |
| Stamina (STA) | Engine |

**Your rating OVR** = average of those seven numbers (rounded).

**A player’s squad OVR** = average of every teammate rating they have received (each stat is averaged first, then OVR is calculated).  
**Unrated** = nobody has rated them yet. They do not get a peer OVR until the first rating lands.

You cannot rate yourself. Your own card is hidden from your rating list.

## In 5 steps

### 1. Open your squad
Go to **Newtown Sporting Club**:  
https://kick-draft.vercel.app/newtown-sporting-club

Tap **Claim / rate**.

### 2. Sign in with Google
Use **Continue with Google**. The same Google account is how the app knows who you are.

### 3. Claim your player (once)
Tap **your** card, then confirm.

Rules:

- **One Google account = one player, forever**
- **One player = one owner** — if someone else already claimed that card, pick another
- You cannot switch later yourself
- If you already claimed in another group, you stay with that player (claiming is global, not per group)

After you claim, you are sent to the rate page.

### 4. Rate teammates
On **Rate teammates**, tap **Rate** on a player. Move the seven sliders, then **Save**.

- Sliders start at 50 if you have never rated them
- If you rated them before and the wait is over, **Update** re-opens your last scores
- You can rate as many different teammates as you like

### 5. Two-week wait to rate the same person again
After you save a rating for someone, you **cannot change it for 14 days**. The page shows **Again in X days**.

- The 14 days start from your last save for that teammate
- Other teammates can still be rated immediately
- When the wait ends, tap **Update** and save a new score

---

## Who rated whom

Anyone can open **Who rated whom** (no login):  
https://kick-draft.vercel.app/newtown-sporting-club/reviews

That public list shows ratings from the **last 30 days**, up to **2 rows** per rater → rated pair.

---

## Tips

- Claim the real you — it is permanent
- Be honest; peer averages drive team balance
- Unrated cards show **Unrated** until the first teammate rates them
- A captain / super admin can unclaim a card if someone claimed the wrong player. Past ratings that person gave stay in the system
- Super admin can remove a rating; that pair can rate again immediately
